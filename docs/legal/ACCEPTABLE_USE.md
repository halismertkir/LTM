# Acceptable use

LTM is a tool for remembering **your own** conversations with the knowledge of the
people in them. The license governs what you may do with the code; this page states what
the project considers legitimate use of the product, what contributors will and will not
build, and what the hosted service does not allow.

## Do not use LTM to

- Record or monitor anyone covertly — partners, family members, employees, colleagues,
  tenants, students, customers, the public.
- Record where the law requires consent that you have not obtained, or after someone has
  objected.
- Record children without the consent of their guardians, or in places where people
  undress, receive care, or otherwise expect privacy.
- Collect evidence against, profile, score, discipline or track individuals.
- Capture conversations you are bound to keep confidential and send them to a
  third-party AI provider.
- Identify people by their voice, infer sensitive characteristics about them, or build
  dossiers on third parties.
- Circumvent a meeting platform's or venue's recording rules or indicators.

## What the project will not accept

Contributions that hide or suppress capture indicators, bypass or weaken consent
enforcement, add stealth or remote-activation modes, exfiltrate data to destinations the
user did not configure, or add biometric speaker identification without a prior design
discussion about consent.

## If you see misuse

LTM is self-hosted; the authors cannot see or stop individual deployments. If someone is
recording you without your consent, the remedy is with that person and, where
applicable, with your local authorities or data-protection regulator. Abuse of the hosted
service can be reported to its operator.
