LTM is **source-available**. You can read, run, modify and share the code for
noncommercial purposes. Commercial use needs a paid commercial license.

This page is a plain-language summary. The binding terms are in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)
(PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0) and, for commercial users, in the commercial
agreement you sign with the licensor.

## At a glance

| You want to… | Allowed under `LICENSE`? |

| Run it for your household | Yes |

| Bundle it into something you sell, or use it to deliver paid services | **No — commercial license required** |

If you are unsure whether your use is commercial, assume it is and ask.

operated by the licensor. The Enterprise plan includes the right to use LTM commercially,
either on the hosted service or self-hosted under the terms of that plan.

## Why this model

LTM records and remembers conversations. It should be something a person can run
entirely on their own hardware and audit line by line — so the source is available and
personal use is free. Companies that build on it or deploy it for their staff fund its
development.

## Contributions

Because the project is dual-licensed (noncommercial license here, commercial license for
paying customers), every contribution needs a signed
[Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) so that the licensor is able to offer your
contribution under both. You keep the copyright in what you write. See
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Third-party software

LTM depends on open-source packages that remain under their own licenses (MIT, Apache-2.0,
BSD, ISC, PostgreSQL and similar). The LTM license applies only to the code in this
repository. Model weights, speech-to-text engines and AI APIs that you connect to LTM are
governed by their own terms — including whether *they* may be used commercially.
